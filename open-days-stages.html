Open days and events

Our events are the best way to get to know us. Meet our friendly students and staff, ask any questions you might have, and get tips about anything from applying to uni to life in Brighton.

We run events throughout the year and have something for everyone, wherever you are in the world or whichever stage you're at in your university journey.

Explore the events you can take part in below, and if you’re not sure which is right for you, get in touch with our friendly enquiries team on 01273 644644 – they’ll be happy to help.
